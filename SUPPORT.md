## 3. Reporting Bugs & Issues

If you encountered a bug or unexpected behavior:

1. Search existing [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/10xGraph/10xgraph/issues) across the organization to avoid duplicate reports.
2. If the issue is new, open a [Bug Report](https://github.com/10xGraph/.github/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) with a minimal reproducible Python graph snippet.
3. For security-sensitive issues, refer directly to our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md).
